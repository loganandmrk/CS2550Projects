I really enjoy playing video games. One of the games I recently started is Red Dead Redemption 2. This game has been incredibly fun to play as it puts you into the scene of the wild west as an outlaw. I think its really fun when you get to be part of a world that doesn't exist. I have really enjoyed creating a lot of awesome things on it including: a knight who holds my pens, a to-do list board, and hobby/gaming organization containers. I hope to do a lot more with 3D printing in the future as I continue to develop my engineering skills.
